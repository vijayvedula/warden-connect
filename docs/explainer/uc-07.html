02

The flow

Each step is labelled with which layer owns it. The badge matters more than it looks: most of the confusion about this system is people expecting one layer to answer the other's question.

1
warden-connect

Quarantine

One command, with a reason and an operator. Dual control at tier 1. The registry state becomes terminal — not a flag somebody can flip back.

2
warden-connect

Append to the signed feed

Append-only, contiguously sequenced, each entry signed with the revocation key — separate from the issuer key, so an operator who can mint contracts cannot thereby cut them.

3
warden-connect

Name every connection

The party first, as a backstop that holds even if the contract-set construction has a bug — then each affected cid explicitly, so a mediator does not have to derive the set itself.

4
warden-connect

Fan out

Push is latency only. It moves the median under a couple of seconds instead of under the poll interval. Every failure is reported and none of them changes the guarantee, because mediators pull anyway.

5
warden-connect

Collect acknowledgements

Signed, naming the sequence applied and the in-flight calls aborted. An HTTP 200 from a mediator is not a confirmation: it accepted a notification, which is not the same as having applied the order.

6
warden-connect

Blast radius, as of the cut

Everything the party could reach and everything that could reach it, annotated with business service — because "these three services stop" is what a decision needs, not a list of four hundred ids.

7
warden-connect

Emit signed SETs

Downstream systems and federated partners cut their own sessions, including ones that have never heard of warden-connect.

03

Where the boundary is

Two layers, and neither can widen the other. Removing one does not silently loosen what remains.

warden-connect

Cuts the relationship

Revocation is relationship-layer: the contract stops existing, so there is nothing left for any call to be inside. This is what makes containment one action rather than a hunt.

connect quarantine --reason …
Warden core

Stops seeing calls at all

Core is not asked to deny anything. The mediator refuses the connection before core is reached — containment by removing the boundary, not by tightening it.

04

The exception paths

Where the real design lives. A main flow describes the day everything works; these describe the days that decide whether anyone keeps the thing switched on.

A1A mediator is unreachable

Reported as not confirmed, never assumed benign — and the report states the bound: that mediator applies the cut within its own poll interval regardless. An unconfirmed mediator is not an unbounded risk, and saying so precisely is more useful than either "contained" or "unknown".

A2Quarantine would break a critical service

The impacted service list is surfaced with the decision. Containment is not silently downgraded: an override is an explicit, dual-controlled, logged act.

A3Clearing quarantine

Requires full re-admission, not a state flip. You cannot restore a party you have not re-proved — and restoring should be harder than cutting.
